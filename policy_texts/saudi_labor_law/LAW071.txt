Law ID: LAW071
Category: Contract Termination
Article: 74
Title: Cases Ending the Employment Contract
Rule: The employment contract ends by: (1) agreement of both parties, provided the worker consents in writing; (2) expiry of the contract term, unless it was expressly or implicitly renewed; (3) the wish of either party in an indefinite-term contract, subject to Article 75; (4) the worker reaching retirement age; (5) force majeure; (6) permanent closure of the establishment; (7) termination of the activity in which the worker is employed.
Conditions: Applies to all employment contracts governed by the Labor Law.
Exceptions: Termination by agreement is valid only with the worker's written consent.
