Law ID: LAW007
Category: Wages
Article: 91
Title: Deduction for Damage
Rule: An employer may deduct an amount needed to repair damage caused by an employee's fault or violation of instructions, subject to statutory limits.
Conditions: Damage must result from the employee's fault or violation and not force majeure or another person's fault.
Exceptions: The employee may challenge the deduction through the competent labor court.
