Law ID: LAW074
Category: Contract Termination
Article: 77
Title: Compensation for Termination Without a Legitimate Reason
Rule: If a contract is terminated for an illegitimate reason, the injured party is entitled to compensation: 15 days' wage for each year of service in an indefinite-term contract, or the wage for the remaining term in a fixed-term contract. The compensation may not be less than two months' wage.
Conditions: Applies when the contract does not specify a compensation amount for illegitimate termination.
Exceptions: A contractually agreed compensation amount applies if specified.
