Law ID: LAW080
Category: End of Service
Article: 88
Title: Settlement of Entitlements at End of Service
Rule: When the employment relationship ends, the employer must pay the worker's wages and entitlements within a maximum of one week from the end of the relationship. If the worker terminated the contract, the employer must settle all entitlements within two weeks.
Conditions: Applies to all contract endings.
Exceptions: The employer may deduct any debt owed by the worker from the entitlements within the legal limits.
