Law ID: LAW079
Category: End of Service
Article: 87
Title: Full End-of-Service Award Cases
Rule: Notwithstanding Article 85, the worker is entitled to the full end-of-service award if they leave work due to force majeure beyond their control. A female worker is also entitled to the full award if she terminates the contract within six months of marriage or within three months of giving birth.
Conditions: Applies to the force majeure, marriage and childbirth cases stated in the article.
Exceptions: Overrides the resignation reductions in Article 85.
