Law ID: LAW078
Category: End of Service
Article: 85
Title: End-of-Service Award on Resignation
Rule: If the contract ends by the worker's resignation, the worker is entitled to: no award for service of less than two consecutive years; one third of the award for service of two to less than five years; two thirds of the award for service of five to less than ten years; and the full award for ten years or more.
Conditions: Applies when the worker resigns.
Exceptions: Article 87 cases grant the full award regardless of service length.
