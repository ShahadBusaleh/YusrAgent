Law ID: LAW073
Category: Contract Termination
Article: 76
Title: Compensation for Not Observing Notice
Rule: A party that does not observe the notice period must pay the other party compensation equal to the worker's wage for the notice period or the remainder of it.
Conditions: Applies when an indefinite-term contract is ended without the full notice required by Article 75.
Exceptions: The parties may agree otherwise.
